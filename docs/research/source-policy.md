# Research source policy

Every research batch follows this policy and [licensing.md](licensing.md).
A catalogue entry describes one exact product mode and version interval. An
import feature is not proof of mnemonic creation. Shared words do not prove
shared derivation semantics. Conflicts remain visible as versioned claims or
an evidence-backed `blocked` result; priority never erases contradictory facts.

## Evidence priority contract

This table is exercised by `tests.catalog.test_licensing` against the validator.
"Primary" means eligible to satisfy the necessary primary-evidence gate, not
sufficient to prove the whole record. There are exactly four evidence levels.

| Priority | source_type | Primary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | official-specification | yes |
| 2 | official-documentation | yes |
| 3 | official-source | yes |
| 4 | public-test-vector | no |

## Provenance and reproducibility

1. **Official specification:** cite its publisher, exact section and normative
   version/revision. Pin a commit where available; for mutable text capture the
   retrieved byte SHA-256 and date in the research note.
2. **Official product documentation:** cite the vendor URL, exact heading,
   platform, product version and mode. Record the published revision, or a dated
   snapshot identified by byte SHA-256 when the page has no immutable revision.
3. **Official source at a pinned commit:** establish upstream ownership, use a
   permalink to the relevant file/lines and record the full lowercase Git object
   ID (40 or 64 hex characters). A branch name, moving tag or short hash is not
   a pin. Record file/archive SHA-256 in the accompanying note when extracting
   bytes; document transformations and tool versions.
4. **Reproducible public vector:** identify the independent publisher, pinned
   source, vector/file SHA-256, inputs, expected output, command, tool versions
   and observed result. Use only public or clearly synthetic non-funded data.
   This corroborates a claim but cannot alone promote it to `verified`.

Every evidence JSON contains the exact HTTPS `url`, `revision`, UTC date
`verified_on` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), a narrowly scoped `claim`, and `record_ids` naming
the records it supports. Each record points back through `evidence_ids`;
license decisions additionally use `decision_evidence`, and vectors use
`test_vector_ids`. Put details that do not fit those fields in a linked research
note; do not invent schema fields. Record a fresh date only after actual review.
Source and license revisions must match the material being assessed.

Dictionary `sha256` hashes the exact committed UTF-8 word-list bytes, including
line endings and final newline, before any normalization. Record upstream byte
hashes separately if bytes were transformed. Explain source order, normalization,
count, collisions and position rules, and reproduce their checks offline.
Hashes identify content; they do not prove authenticity, licensing or approval.

## No circular evidence

Generated catalogue pages, our own summary, a prior `verified` label, or a vector
whose expected output was generated by the implementation under test cannot
establish an upstream fact. Follow citations to an independent primary source;
mirrors and repeated summaries are discovery aids, not extra votes. A legal
decision must cite the actual applicable license and its scope, not another
record's positive flag. Cross-record backlinks provide traceability, not proof.
Never relabel a public vector as official evidence to pass validation.

## Enforcement boundary

`verified` dictionaries, schemes and wallets require at least one schema-valid
level 1–3 item with a matching backlink. Missing, malformed and vector-only
evidence fails `primary-evidence`; a malformed official-source commit also fails
`unpinned-evidence`. Schema checks enforce fields, dates and commit syntax;
semantic checks enforce references and dictionary byte/count integrity. The
validator cannot authenticate upstream ownership, interpret claims or licenses,
reproduce arbitrary vectors, or detect circular reasoning hidden in prose.
Those are mandatory reviewer duties before setting positive decisions.

The `example.invalid` sources under `tests/catalog/fixtures` simulate evidence
types only. They assert no real source, legal clearance or product support and
must never be copied into the production catalogue as research evidence.
